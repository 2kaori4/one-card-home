## About

### Small slips happen every day

Did I unplug the iron? Did I lock the door? Little worries like these follow us at home. One Card Home helps.

- Made for the small slips of daily home life
- Cards open by time of day: before leaving, back home, before bed
- Only one card shows at a time, so it feels light
- Warm cream paper-card look, easy to use every day

### How it works

Open the card for your time of day. Tap Done or Later, and the next card comes up.

- Tap "Done ✓" to move on, or "Later" to see it again in 5, 30, or 60 minutes
- Turn cards on or off, add your own cards, and undo a step
- Finish all cards to see confetti and a "Tonight is all set!" message
- A streak and a weekly view show your progress; switch Japanese or English

### A calmer day, one card at a time

The app is built to be opened every day. Checking little things can become a simple habit.

- Fewer worries when you leave home and when you go to sleep
- Short daily tips and kind messages keep it friendly
- Next: more cards and more ways to fit each person's home life
